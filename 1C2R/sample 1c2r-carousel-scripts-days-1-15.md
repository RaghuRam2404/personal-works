# 1Crore2Reps — Carousel Scripts, Days 1–15

Source strategy: `1c2r Carousel-Only Instagram Funnel.md` (funnel, series, templates, CTA ladder) and `brand_guideline.md` (voice, visual system, pricing, founder rules). This document writes out the first 15 scheduled carousels — Weeks 1–3 of the funnel's own **Four-week calendar** (Recognition → Drift → Recovery) — as ready-to-design, ready-to-caption posts.

## How to read this document

- **"Day"** = the Nth scheduled carousel, not a calendar date. Publishing stays on the funnel's fixed rhythm: **Mon / Tue / Wed / Fri / Sun**, five per week, so Days 1–15 span three real weeks.
- Each day keeps the **Weekly publishing system**'s fixed day → series mapping (Monday = Drift Report, Tuesday = Accountability Autopsy, Wednesday = Two-Rep Challenge, Friday = Inside 1C2R or Rep Receipt, Sunday = Stop Restarting), so the account rhythm stays legible to followers.
- Two judgment calls, flagged where they happen:
  - **Day 11** ("The perfect plan creates the next restart") is listed under the Problem topic list in the source doc but landed on a Monday in the Four-week calendar. It's written here as a **Reach** scene (recognition, not diagnosis) to preserve the Monday = Drift Report rhythm; Day 12 carries the "why" diagnosis.
  - **Day 9 vs Day 14**: the source doc's Friday rotation rule (Mechanism/Proof alternating) conflicts with its own Four-week calendar. This document follows the calendar's actual topics: Day 9 (Week 2 Friday) is Mechanism, Day 14 (Week 3 Friday) is Proof — which also happens to match the "Rep Receipt" title already on that slot.
- **Day 14 is founder proof, not member proof.** No real member receipts exist this early, and the brand's non-negotiables forbid fabricating testimonials. Swap in a real, anonymized member Rep Receipt as soon as one exists.
- **Day 15's price slide** should show **$29/month (founding, first 50 seats)** if that window is still open when this is actually published, even though the source topic title says "$49" (standard post-founding rate). Both numbers are shown on the slide for that reason.
- **Master comment keyword:** `REP`, used on every Participation post. Do not invent a new keyword per post.
- **Default visual system** (applies unless a note says otherwise): black background `#000000`, white primary text `#FFFFFF`, one decisive word/number per carousel in accent `#34C363`, bold condensed headline font, legible sans body font, portrait canvas **1080×1350**, wordmark on the final slide, slide-number indicator throughout. Assets to pull from `brand assets/`: `panda_logo.png`, `1c2r black banner.png`, `1c2c words.png`, `1c2r small.png`.
- **Panda appears on 4 of 15 carousels** (Days 3, 8, 13, 15 ≈ 27%), matching the 20–30% rule. Every other carousel is Panda-free by design — proof and mechanism content in particular should stay human, not mascot-fronted.

### Day 3 · Wednesday · Two-Rep Challenge · Participation

**Slides**

1. **Challenge hook** — PICK YOUR TWO REPS FOR THIS WEEK.
2. **Define Money Rep** — One measurable money action you will repeat this week.
3. **Define Muscle Rep** — One measurable movement action you will repeat this week.
4. **Strong examples** — “Review spending every Friday, 15 minutes.” “Walk 6,000 steps, 5 days.”
5. **Weak examples to avoid** — “Be better with money.” “Get fit.” Too vague to report.
6. **Ask for comment** — Comment REP with your one money action and one muscle action.
7. **Promise human response** — A real human replies — not a bot.

**Caption**

> A goal without a number attached is just a mood.
>
> “Save more” never once made me open my banking app. “Move ₹5,000 to the index fund every 1st” did.
>
> What’s the smallest version of your goal you could actually report on Friday?
>
> Comment REP with one money action and one muscle action. A real human reply will help make them measurable.

**CTA:** Comment REP with both actions.
**Panda:** Yes — holding a “REP” card next to the strong/weak examples, pointing at the strong column.
**Primary metric:** Qualified REP comments, DMs.

## Production checklist before publishing any of the above

- [ ] One idea per slide; cover ≤10 words; interior slides ≤25 words.
- [ ] Exactly one word/phrase per carousel carries the `#34C363` accent — pick the sharpest word on the hook or CTA slide.
- [ ] Wordmark (`1c2r black banner.png` or `1c2c words.png`) appears on the final slide.

- [ ] Day 3, 8, 13 captions all drive to the same comment keyword: `REP`.
- [ ] Day 14 is clearly labeled founder/building-in-public content, not a member testimonial.
