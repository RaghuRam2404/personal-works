## Week-by-week

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Set up X + fix the site
- **Day 1:** Create @dadfit on X. Bio: *"Engineer-dad. Lost 34 kg. Now I help other Indian dads get their body and energy back. NASM certified. → dadfit.in"* Add your NASM cert as a pinned reply for credibility.
- **Day 1:** Make the 4 site fixes above. Also add one sentence under the hero: *"Free 3-min assessment · No signup wall · Get your specific blockers."*
- **Day 2:** Start posting on X. 1 post/day + 10 replies/day to relevant accounts (Indian dad-Twitter, finance Twitter, health-adjacent creators, Indian founders).
- **Day 2:** Set up a Zoho CRM stage pipeline: `New Lead → Assessment Completed → Booked Call → Attended Call → Paid → Refunded`. This is your only dashboard for the next 60 days.
- **Day 3–7:** Post daily. Reply daily. Do not sell yet. Just get your first 20 X followers who are actual dads.

**Cost this week:** ₹0

### Week 2 (Days 8–14): Content sprint + first calls
- **Post 7 more times on X.** Follow the pillar template below.
- **Post one link to your assessment quiz** on X (day 10). Track opens in Zoho.
- **Message every person who fills the assessment** within 2 hours: *"Thanks for filling this. Want a free 20-min call to walk through your results?"* Use WhatsApp — you have their number.
- **Goal this week:** 5 assessment completions, 3 booked calls.

**Cost this week:** ₹0

### Week 3 (Days 15–21): First 10 free calls — listen, don't sell
- Do 10 free 30-min calls with dads who filled the assessment. Zoom or Google Meet.
- **Ask, don't pitch:** (1) What have you tried before? (2) What broke? (3) What would you pay someone to actually solve this for you? (4) What's stopping you from doing that today?
- Record every objection in Zoho CRM as a note on the lead.
- After every call, ask: *"If I built a 12-month program that solves exactly this for dads like you, what would you expect to pay?"* Write down every answer.
- **Do not sell.** You are collecting language and price signal.

**Cost this week:** ₹0

### Week 4 (Days 22–28): Write your offer
- Based on the 10 calls, write your offer on **one page**. Use `contact.html` as the template — turn it into `/foundations` with:
  - **Headline:** Use the exact words dads used on calls. Not your words.
  - **What it is:** 12-month coaching. Group cohort of 20 dads. Weekly live call. WhatsApp support. Monthly 1:1 review. App-based workouts.
  - **What it isn't:** A quick fix. A transformation challenge. A diet plan.
  - **Price:** ₹49,500 one-time (or ₹4,500/month × 12 via Razorpay). **Founding member price. Rises to ₹99,000 after 20 members.**
  - **Guarantee:** 14-day money-back.
  - **CTA:** *"Reply to this to hold your seat"* + WhatsApp link.
- Set up Razorpay payment link (₹0 setup, 2.15% + GST per transaction). No integration needed — just a link.
- Add the `/foundations` link to the top nav and homepage.

**Cost this week:** ₹0

### Week 5 (Days 29–35): Sell 3 founding members
- **Post on X:** *"Opening 20 founding member seats for DadFit Foundations — 12 months of coaching for Indian dads. ₹49,500 (half of what it'll cost after these 20). If you're a dad 30–50 who's tried and failed, reply."*
- **Send WhatsApp to every past assessment lead** who is age 30–50 with the same offer.
- **Send 20 personal DMs on X** to dads who've engaged with your posts.
- **Do 5–10 more free calls** — this time you're selling.
- **Target:** 3 paid founding members by day 35. That's ₹1,48,500 revenue.
- Deliver via WhatsApp + Google Meet + a shared Google Doc for their program. **No Trainerize, no Circle.** Ugly is fine. Learn what they need before you buy tools.

**Cost this week:** ₹0 (Razorpay only charges per transaction)

### Week 6 (Days 36–42): Deliver + document
- Onboard 3 members: 1 welcome WhatsApp voice note in first 24h, 1 baseline 30-min 1:1 call in first 7 days.
- **First live cohort call on day 40** (30 min, Google Meet, 3 attendees + you).
- Document everything you say on that call → this becomes your coach playbook.
- On X: post 2 lessons from the coaching week (anonymized). This is your best content — real, specific, from the trenches.

**Cost this week:** ₹0

### Week 7 (Days 43–49): Turn on paid ads
- **You now have 3 paying customers and 30–40 pieces of X content.** Time to test paid.
- Meta ads (not X — cheaper in India, median CPM $1.35 [Superads India](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/india)): 3 ad creatives (rewrite your best-performing X posts as image + text ads), ₹500/day for 14 days = **₹7,000**.
- Target: **males 30–50, Bengaluru/Mumbai/Delhi/Hyderabad/Pune, interests: fitness + parenting + Indian finance/business news.**
- Point ads to your assessment quiz (`dadfit.in/assessment.html`) — you already have the funnel built.
- Weekly review: **kill any ad above ₹1,000 CPL.** Scale any ad below ₹500 CPL.

**Cost this week:** ₹3,500 (first half of ad budget)

### Week 8 (Days 50–60): Second cohort + decide
- Continue X posting (habit is locked in by now).
- Continue Meta ads (₹3,500 spent this half).
- **Sell 3–5 more founding members** using ad-driven assessment leads.
- On day 55: fractional CA consult (1 hour, ~₹3,000). Ask: GST registration timing, payment gateway setup, invoice format, expense categories.
- **Day 60 decision meeting with yourself:**
  - If you have 5+ paid members and CPL under ₹800 → **scale.** Open the next 15 founding seats at ₹99,000. Move to the v2 growth tree.
  - If you have 3–4 paid members and CPL ₹800–1,500 → **iterate.** Rewrite the offer or targeting for another 30 days.
  - If you have 0–2 paid members → **stop paid ads.** Do 20 more free calls to figure out why. The offer or the audience is wrong.

**Cost this week:** ₹5,500 (₹3,500 ads + ₹3,000 CA) — **total 60-day spend: ~₹9,000. Under budget.**
