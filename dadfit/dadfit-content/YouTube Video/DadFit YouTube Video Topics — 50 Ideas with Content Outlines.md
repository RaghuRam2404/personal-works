# DadFit YouTube Video Topics — 50 Ideas with Content Outlines

## Overview

This report presents 50 YouTube video topic ideas for DadFit, systematically derived from the audience pain points, keywords, traits, and subniches of **salaried Indian fathers aged 30–45**. Topics span the three core DadFit pillars — **Physical Fitness (Workout + Diet), Mental Fitness, and Financial Fitness** — aligned with the content allocation framework: Workout 35%, Diet 35%, Mental 25%, Financial 5%. Each topic is mapped to the target persona archetype and the core pain points it addresses.[^1]
