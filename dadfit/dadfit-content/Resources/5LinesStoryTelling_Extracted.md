# The Five Line Story Method

## The Problem with Planning

I used to spend hours trying to plan a video and still have no idea what I was actually trying to say. I would make an outline, rewrite, scrap it, and start all over again. Or I'd skip it all and just start filming, hoping I would figure it all out in the edit. But then I'd be staring at a messy timeline with no clue what the story was actually supposed to be.

I was overthinking, and totally getting lost in the details... until I started doing something so stupidly simple I couldn't believe it actually worked.

**Five lines. That's it. And suddenly every story clicked.**

Most comments I get are about my storytelling. So today, I'll show you a little secret — because when you've written down these five lines, you've got a blueprint to create a story and a script that actually makes people *feel* something. Something with a heart.

***

## Why the Core of Your Story Matters

Before YouTube, I ran my own video agency with a friend. We created commercials, series, small documentaries — I always loved doing crazy stuff, weird transitions, wild concepts. And I think it made our work a lot more awesome.

But what I kept bumping into, over and over again — whether you're alone or working with a big crew — filmmaking is a **lot** of decision making. And in that chaos, you start losing the core of the story.

> **The core of your story is one of the most important things.** If you don't know what the story is really about, no editing trick or camera trick is going to save you.

***

## The Five Line Story Method

The only thing you have to do is write five lines.

### Line 1 — Situation
**Where are we and what's happening?**
The starting point. Set the world we're in and what's going on.

### Line 2 — Desire
**What does the character want?**
What do they want to obtain or reach? This is the goal driving the story.

### Line 3 — Conflict
**What gets in the way?**
The tension or obstacle that makes it hard to reach the desire.

### Line 4 — Change
**What shifts things?**
A turning point — big or small — a different way of seeing something. Growth.

### Line 5 — Result
**How does it end?**
The new reality. How is the situation different from where we started?

***

## Example: The Lion King

| Line | Content |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | Simba is a young prince, excited to one day rule the Pride Lands. |
| **Desire** | He wants to prove he's brave and worthy of being a king. |
| **Conflict** | After his father's death, Simba runs away — ashamed and afraid to face his past. |
| **Change** | With help from his friends and a vision from his father, Simba returns to confront Scar. |
| **Result** | He restores balance to the Pride Lands and steps fully into who he was meant to be. |

> Obviously there are missing details — but that *is* the whole story. Once you start using this method, you'll start seeing it in every movie you watch.

***

## Why Each Line Works

- **Situation** — Gives the viewer a clear starting point: who's in the story, what world we're in
- **Desire** — Shows the goal driving the story; sparks curiosity and gets the audience to lean in and *care*
- **Conflict** — Introduces tension or an obstacle; without it, there's no real reason to keep watching
- **Change** — Marks the turning point; shows growth and gives the audience a sense of forward movement
- **Result** — Delivers a satisfying takeaway, a feeling of closure; ties everything back to where we started

> Together, these five lines are just how we naturally process every story.

***

## Important: Five Lines Are a Launchpad

These five lines **aren't your whole video**. They are a launchpad.

- Use them to **kick off** your story and start building
- Come back to them **when the project overwhelms you**
- Check if what you're building connects back to one of your five lines

## Example: Expanding Five Lines to Ten

### Five-Line Version
1. I'm a filmmaker from the Netherlands and I've loved making videos since I was a kid.
2. My dream was to be a YouTuber and share stories from the heart — not just for clients.
3. But every time I tried to start, self-doubt and overthinking pulled me back.
4. When I let go of the YouTube dream, I finally realized I was focusing on the wrong reasons — I just wanted to create stories.
5. So I finally sat down and started, and this video is the result — and that first step pushed me to go all in on this YouTube journey.

### Ten-Line Version
1. I'm a filmmaker from the Netherlands and I've loved making videos since I was a kid.
2. My dream was to be a YouTuber and share stories from the heart — not just for clients.
3. But every time I tried to start, self-doubt and overthinking pulled me back.
4. My mind kept yelling: *you can't go from making ads for big companies to making YouTube videos.*
5. Those fears became excuses — I'm too late, too old, don't have enough skills, gear, or security.
6. So I let go of the big YouTube dream, and at first it felt good and free.
7. But then the same question kept coming back: *why did I actually want to do YouTube all this time?*
8. When I stripped away views, subscribers, and money, I realized it's about the joy of creating and sharing stories.
9. That realization made me push through self-doubt and focus on the process — and the video you're watching is the result of that.
10. It wasn't easy, but I already learned so much, and it pushed me to go all in on YouTube and share every lesson along the way.

> Going from five lines to ten doesn't change the **core** of the story — it just adds richness and detail. You could go to 20, then more, until you have the whole video. **If you can do five lines, you can expand into an entire story.**

## Key Takeaway

Simple is good in filmmaking. These five lines help you see the whole story **before** you get distracted by gear, transitions, voiceovers, color grading — all the details and choices.

**Nail these five lines first → you've got the emotional core. The life thread.**
