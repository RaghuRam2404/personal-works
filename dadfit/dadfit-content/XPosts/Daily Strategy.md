# DadFit X Posting Strategy

## Weekly Content Rotation

| Day | Format | Content prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Story post | “At 96 kg I got winded on a hike in Thailand while older tourists breezed past me. That was 2019. This is what I did in the 6 years since — thread.” |
| Tuesday | Myth post | “The 6-week transformation is a lie for men over 35. Here’s what actually works and why it takes longer.” |
| Wednesday | Implementation | “3 rules for a 20-minute home workout that works even if you slept 5 hours and skipped dinner.” |
| Thursday | If I have a client | “A dad in my cohort just did his first pull-up at 42. He’s been working on it for 8 months. That’s the actual timeline for men our age.” |
| Thursday | Dad Reality Check | Poll, objection, “what have you tried?”, or constraint-research question |
| Friday | Founder voice | “I’m a software engineer who got fat. Here’s what my job taught me about my body that trainers never mention.” |
| Saturday | Deep thread | “Why 30–50-year-old Indian dads regain weight after every transformation — and the 4 habits that break the cycle. Thread.” |
| Sunday | Invitation | “I’m opening 3 more seats in DadFit Foundations this week. Reply if you’re a dad 30–50 who has tried and failed. I’ll DM.” |
| Sunday | Founder Experiment / Proof Log | A transparent weekly system, measurement, failure, adjustment, or personal protocol |

## Content Guardrails

Do not use:

- Marriage-related content.
- “Perfect dad” theatre or performative family content.
- Crying reels or emotionally manipulative transformation footage.
- Generic motivational quotes.

## Compliance Note

Every transformation claim must include an **“Individual results vary”** footer to remain ASCI-safe.
