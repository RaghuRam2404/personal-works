# Intake Questions — Landing Page Builder

Run these questions before writing any copy. Each question maps to a specific page section. Ask them conversationally — one at a time if the user seems uncertain, as a checklist if they're experienced.

---

## Block 1: Business & Audience Context

**Q1. What is your business / product / service?**
- Goal: understand the domain, vocabulary, and sophistication level
- If vague (e.g. "coaching"), follow up: "What specific problem do you help people solve?"

**Q2. Who is your ideal audience for this landing page?**
- Push for specificity: role, life stage, level of sophistication
- Bad answer: "small business owners" → Good answer: "service-based solopreneurs with 0–5 employees who have been in business 1–3 years"
- Follow-up if generic: "If you could describe the single person most likely to take this quiz, who are they?"

**Q3. What is this person already doing or trying that isn't working?**
- This uncovers the "even though" for the frustration hook
- Example: "They're posting on social media every day but not getting leads"
- Follow-up: "What effort are they already making that should be producing results?"

**Q4. What result does this person desperately want?**
- This unlocks both the results hook and the value proposition
- Push for a specific, measurable outcome: not "better sleep" → "7+ hours of uninterrupted sleep per night"
- Follow-up: "If this quiz worked perfectly for them, what would their life look like 90 days from now?"

---

## Block 2: Hook Selection

**Q5. Which hook type feels more true to your audience?**

> Option A — **Frustration hook**: The audience is already trying but stuck. They want to diagnose *why*.
> Option B — **Results/readiness hook**: The audience is aspiring. They want to know if they're *ready*.

If unsure, use this decision rule:
- Are they currently in the problem and frustrated? → Frustration hook
- Have they not started yet and are curious/hopeful? → Results hook
- Both? → Create two variants and A/B test

**Q6. (If frustration hook chosen) Complete this sentence:**
> "Feeling frustrated that _______ even though you _______?"

Help them fill in the blanks based on Q3.

**Q7. (If results hook chosen) Complete this sentence:**
> "Are you ready to _______?"

Use the specific desired outcome from Q4.

---

## Block 3: Value Proposition

**Q8. What are the 3 specific areas your assessment will measure and help them improve?**
- These must be things the audience *wants* — not just things you assess
- Frame as outcomes: "sleep quality" not "sleep tracking"
- If they give more than 3, ask: "Which 3 would matter most to your audience?"
- If they can't name 3, offer to generate options based on their niche (see landing-page-structure.md for examples)

---

## Block 4: Credibility

**Q9. Who created this assessment? Tell me about them.**
- What is their name, title, or role?
- What is their most impressive credential or track record? (clients served, revenue generated, years of experience, companies built)
- Have they published anything — a book, podcast, course, research?

**Q10. Is there any data, research, or statistics you can reference?**
- Internal data: "In 5 years of coaching, I've found that 80% of clients struggle with X before they come to me"
- External data: industry reports, academic studies, survey results
- If none: "Would you like me to suggest relevant statistics from published research in your niche?"

**Q11. Do you have any social proof?**
- Number of people who've taken the assessment previously
- Testimonial quotes from clients
- Logos of companies or publications
- If none: skip social proof block initially, add after launch

---

## Block 5: CTA & Mechanics

**Q12. What does the user do after clicking the CTA?**
- Most common: start a quiz/assessment (ScoreApp, Typeform, Outgrow, etc.)
- Other options: book a call, download a report, register for a webinar
- This determines the CTA button verb and micro-copy

**Q13. How long does it take to complete?**
- Standard: 3–5 minutes for a 15-question assessment
- Must be accurate — underestimating erodes trust

**Q14. Is it free?**
- Confirm this is stated prominently in the CTA
- If there is a paid tier, clarify what the free version includes

**Q15. What do they get immediately after completing it?**
- Instant score/results page? (most effective)
- PDF report sent to email?
- Booking link for a strategy call?
- The immediacy of the reward is the strongest lever in the CTA

---

## Block 6: Brand & Design

**Q16. Does a brand profile already exist?**
- Yes → load it via the **brand-strategy** skill
- No → pause and run the **brand-strategy** skill now before writing any copy
- Questions to answer if no profile exists: primary color, secondary color, font style preference (modern/minimal vs. bold/energetic vs. professional/corporate), tone of voice (authoritative, warm, urgent, playful, conversational)

**Q17. What is the primary platform / context this page will live on?**
- Standalone domain (e.g. myquiz.com)
- Embedded on existing website
- Shared via social media link
- Used in paid ad campaigns
- This affects the design brief for ui-ux-designer

**Q18. Any competitor pages or design inspiration?**
- Ask for URLs or describe the aesthetic
- This is input for the ui-ux-designer brief

---

## Block 7: Stretch Questions (ask if time allows)

**Q19. What is the #1 objection someone would have before clicking the CTA?**
- "It'll take too long" → address in CTA micro-copy
- "What do you do with my data?" → add trust signal near email capture
- "I'm not sure this is for me" → tighten the audience specificity in the hook

**Q20. What is the goal conversion rate for this page?**
- Benchmark: 20–40% of visitors clicking through to the quiz
- If they have an existing page, what is the current rate?
- This sets the bar for evaluating the design

**Q21. Will you run paid traffic to this page?**
- If yes: the hook must match the ad creative exactly (message match)
- Ask what the ad hook/headline is so the landing page mirrors it
